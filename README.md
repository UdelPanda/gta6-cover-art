Vice City Case Files — a web app that turns your photo into GTA 6-style cover art. Multi-panel photographic covers (hero + three acts), a WANTED case file with your name, alias and wanted stars, four story templates, a comic filter that redraws any photo as an illustrated panel, and PNG export in three post sizes (1080×1080, 1080×1350, 1920×1080). Everything runs locally in your browser; photos never leave your machine.

## Features

- **Multi-panel covers** — hero + three-act layouts (Hero, Grid, Trio, Solo) with your photo in every panel
- **WANTED case file** — your name, a generated alias, wanted stars and a stats line on a police case-file band
- **Story templates** — Heist, Getaway, Night Drive and Beach Day, each setting the panels into three acts
- **Comic filter** — posterize + bold outline turns any photo into an illustrated GTA-style panel
- **Four sample photos** — rooftop terrace, jet ski, marina yacht and gas station, all image-model-made
- **Export** — PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 and 1920×1080, all three at once

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
